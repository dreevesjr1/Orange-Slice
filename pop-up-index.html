Welcome to Orange Slice Golf

Golf has history. Tradition. Generations of stories passed down. But for newcomers, it can feel like pressure — rules, expectations, and standards you’re “supposed” to know. Orange Slice Golf exists to break that barrier. We’re a lifestyle community built for beginners, casual players, and anyone curious about the game. No judgment. No scorecards. Just people learning, swinging, and having fun. We host meetups across Orlando — putting greens, simulators, driving ranges, and local courses — giving you a chance to learn the game at your own pace while connecting with great people. Whether you’ve never swung a club or you’re rediscovering the game, you’re welcome here. Grab a club, take a swing, and be part of a community that’s rewriting what golf looks like.
